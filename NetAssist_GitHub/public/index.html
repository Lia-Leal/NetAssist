How it works

From "it is not working" to a next step

  1. Choose your problem

    No technical words needed. Pick what you are experiencing.

  2. Watch the tests run

    Adapter, router, internet, DNS, and more, live on screen.

  3. Read the explanation

    Rules turn raw numbers into a plain-language cause and a short list of fixes.

  4. Fix, re-test, compare

    See a before and after. Every report is saved to your history and analytics.

Built for beginners

Made for students and everyday users with limited networking knowledge. Every result explains itself.

Safe by design

NetAssist only reads and measures. It never changes your network settings and is not a replacement for Wireshark or Packet Tracer.
